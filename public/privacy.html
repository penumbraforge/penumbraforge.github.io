Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

What We Collect

LockScreen AI processes your text prompts to generate wallpaper images. Prompts are sent to our image generation API provider for processing and are not stored on our servers after generation is complete.

Local Storage

Generated wallpapers and your preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your gallery or usage data to any server.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store payment information.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as total generations) to improve the app. No personally identifiable information is collected.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party AI image generation APIs to process prompts. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share any personal information with them beyond the text prompt you provide.

Photos Access

LockScreen AI requests permission to save wallpapers to your photo library. This access is only used when you explicitly choose to save an image. We never read or access your existing photos.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at privacy@penumbraforge.com
