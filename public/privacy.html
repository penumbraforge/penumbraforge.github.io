Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Overview

Penumbra Forge is a privacy-first security studio. We build tools, labs, and open source software designed to respect your privacy by default. This policy covers penumbraforge.com and all services we operate.

What We Don't Collect

The vast majority of our tools run entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Input you provide to browser-based tools (JSON, hashes, passwords, etc.)
  • Lab progress, scores, or responses
  • Personal information, accounts, or login credentials (we have no accounts)
  • Cookies for tracking or advertising

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies, does not track individual visitors, and does not collect personal data. It provides aggregate page view counts only. No data is shared with third parties for advertising.

Edge Workers

A small number of tools (such as Site Score) use Cloudflare Workers to make requests on your behalf — for example, fetching HTTP headers from a URL you provide. These requests are processed in real time and not logged or stored.

Open Source Products

Our open source tools (Gate, mcp-librarian) run locally on your machine. They do not phone home, collect telemetry, or transmit data to Penumbra Forge or any third party. Their source code is publicly auditable on GitHub.

Third-Party Services

The site uses:

  • GitHub Pages — hosting (subject to GitHub's privacy statement)
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DNS, and privacy-focused analytics
  • Google Fonts — font delivery (subject to Google's privacy policy)

Data Retention

We do not retain user data because we do not collect it. Any data you enter into our tools exists only in your browser's memory and is discarded when you close the page.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services evolve. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at privacy@penumbraforge.com
